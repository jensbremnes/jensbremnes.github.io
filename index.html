About

I am an Associate Professor in drone technology and autonomous systems at the Department of Technology and Safety, UiT The Arctic University of Norway.

I hold a PhD in marine cybernetics from NTNU, and have previously worked on AUV autonomy at the Norwegian Defence Research Establishment (FFI) and on seabed and terrain mapping at GeoNord.

Interests

  • Autonomy for robotics — planning, decision-making, adaptive behaviors, mission execution and supervision
  • Risk management in robotics — risk analysis, probabilistic AI (Bayesian methods, Gaussian processes), supervisory control, and formal safety guarantees
  • Field robotics — real-world implementation and deployments, including Arctic operations
  • Multi-vehicle coordination — collaborative control and communication for multiple autonomous systems
  • Ocean mapping & monitoring — sampling strategies for acoustic, optical and chemical sensing
  • Sensor & data fusion — integration of data from multiple sensors and data sources for navigation and situational awareness
