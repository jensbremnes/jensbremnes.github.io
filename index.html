About

Drone technology, from seabed to sky

I'm Associate Professor at UiT The Arctic University of Norway, Department of Technology and Safety, working on drone technology and remotely piloted systems.

I hold a PhD in marine cybernetics from NTNU, where I developed supervisory risk control for autonomous underwater vehicles operating under Arctic sea ice, with research stays at UC Berkeley and UNIS in Svalbard.

At the Norwegian Defence Research Establishment (FFI), I built autonomous planning algorithms for HUGIN-class AUVs, used in sonar surveying, optical inspection, and adaptive sampling — all validated in sea trials. I have also worked on seabed and terrain mapping at GeoNord in Alta.
